Tonnage share of each included row appears here.
Tonnes and % are linked. Type tonnes and % is worked out, or type % and tonnes are worked out. Tonnes also accept sums such as 7884+4307. When every weight is entered as %, the % values are used directly against a 100 basis (or against the ESLS tonnage, if entered). The formula check should then read 100%. Moisture comes from the Moisture template, so type it into the highlighted cells. A blank result is left out of that column's average, and the other rows are re-weighted. The column is marked with an asterisk. Your Excel template counts a blank as zero instead. Copy results gives the rows, tonnes, %, and the weighted average in the template's column order.
